# Skill Spec: architecture-decision

**Status:** Draft, not yet validated

## Problem and users

Material technology and architecture choices need explicit criteria and tradeoffs so teams avoid fashion-driven choices and repeated debate.

## Inputs and output

Input: product constraints/spec, repository architecture and operating context, candidate options. Output: a concise decision brief or ADR with context, criteria, alternatives, consequences, recommendation, and revisit conditions.

## Boundaries

Do not choose based on popularity alone, invent scale/compliance requirements, or make irreversible decisions without surfacing uncertainty. Use current authoritative sources when comparing changing technology capabilities. Do not implement the decision.

## Acceptance and validation

Options are evaluated against explicit constraints and tradeoffs; assumptions are marked; recommendation is traceable. Trial on a real ERP technology decision; record whether decision reduced ambiguity without hiding costs or client-specific constraints.
